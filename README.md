# Pomodoro Zen 🍅🧘

A calm, browser-based Pomodoro timer built for focused study sessions.

## What it does

- **Pomodoro timer:** a classic 25-minute focus session to keep you on track.
- **Concentration sounds:** load an audio file from your computer and it plays on loop, without interruptions.
- **Café-style atmosphere:** a cozy, distraction-free background to help you settle in.

## Why students like it

- **Customizable:** adjust the timer and sound to fit your routine.
- **Your own music:** bring your local tracks and study to your own beat.
- **100% free:** no accounts, no paywalls — anyone can use it.
- **Smart features:** 45% AI-powered, with a background and café theme.

## Availability

Currently available **on the web**. A mobile and desktop app are in active development — stay tuned!
